description: Distinguishes actual FTO academy operations from locally implemented prototype workflows.
---

Phase, training-week, and Limbo extension requests are **not part of the actual academy workflow**. Do not tell POs, FTOs, or Coordinators that FTOs request them as an operational process.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the workflow description and said this is “NOT A THING.” The workspace may contain prototype/local extension controls, but their presence does not make them an academy procedure.

**How to apply:** When explaining the real role-by-role workflow, omit phase-extension requests. If discussing the prototype UI itself, clearly distinguish a local/demo control from actual academy operations.

For the portal's phase-extension workflow, FTO Coordinators initiate extension requests and make the determination. FTOs do not submit extension requests.

**Why:** The user corrected the portal role boundary: FTOs do not request extensions; FTO Coordinators do, and Coordinators make the determination.

**How to apply:** Keep extension-request initiation and determination with the FTO Coordinator role in future portal work. Do not offer FTOs an extension-request action or describe them as requesters.

Substitute FTO coverage requests are a real coordination workflow. They and the Coordinator’s decisions should be shared across accounts and generate notifications; they must not be treated as device-local only.

**Why:** The user confirmed that substitute coverage requests and decisions should sync across accounts and notify the other account.

**How to apply:** For future coverage-request work, design shared persistence and account-to-account notifications for both request submission and Coordinator decisions. Do not extend this requirement to phase or Limbo extensions.
