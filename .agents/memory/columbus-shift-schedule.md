description: User-provided operational rules for shift assignments, case folders, and date formats.

At the beginning of a phase, the FTO opens a case folder for the probationary officer, enters the shift, and selects the days off. The Columbus shift options are A-Co, B-Co, C-Co, DMW, and EMW; “Co” means company. A/B/C company weeks have two days off; DMW and EMW have four working days and three days off. DMW means Day Midwatch (10-hour shifts), and EMW means Evening Midwatch (10-hour shifts). Calendar weeks start Sunday and end Saturday. Company assignment codes pair non-9-prefixed cruiser districts with A/B/C. Midwatch uses only cruiser districts beginning with 9, with D1/D2 for DMW and E1/E2 for EMW; 9-prefixed districts are never A/B/C assignments. Dates must be entered and displayed strictly as MM/DD/YYYY.

**Why:** The user supplied these as the intended Columbus field-training rules.

**How to apply:** Require the FTO to choose two days off for A/B/C or three for DMW/EMW. Generate or validate assignments from the selected patrol area and shift: exclude 9-prefixed districts for A/B/C, and restrict DMW/EMW to 9-prefixed districts with the matching suffix. Carry the selected district-and-shift assignment through every phase form and weekly acknowledgement. Auto-format date entry as MM/DD/YYYY; `?` followed by Enter fills the local current date, and fills date plus time on run entries.

The program is 15 weeks: Phase I is weeks 1–4, Phase II weeks 5–8, Phase III weeks 9–12, and Phase IV weeks 13–15. The user updated Phase I Limbo to cover the entire first week; the 2025 manual’s two-day Phase I rule is outdated. Retain the manual’s current Phase II–IV Limbo lengths (two, one, and one scheduled workdays) unless the user updates them. Limbo days have no SEG ratings, but duty, activity, and run paperwork still applies.

**Why:** The user’s newer Phase I instruction supersedes the 2025 manual, while no update was provided for the other phases.

**How to apply:** Calculate program weeks from the case-folder phase start and selected days off. For Phase I, treat every scheduled workday in week one as Limbo; keep Limbo logs unrated while retaining daily duty, activity, and run documentation.
